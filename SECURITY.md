# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Please do not open a public issue for security problems.

Report them privately through GitHub (Security tab, "Report a vulnerability") or by email to support@hookstep.in with "SECURITY" in the subject. Include what you found, the steps to reproduce it, and the impact you expect.

We aim to acknowledge reports within 5 working days and to keep you updated until the issue is fixed. Once a fix is released we'll credit you in the release notes, unless you'd rather stay anonymous.

## Scope

- This repository (backend, frontend, orchestrator, CLI, SDK, browser extension, MCP server, n8n node).
- The hosted service at hookpost.hookstep.in, tested only against your own account.

Out of scope: denial-of-service or load testing against the hosted service, social engineering, physical attacks, and findings in third-party platforms (X, Meta, LinkedIn and others) that Hookpost connects to.

## Supported versions

Security fixes go to `main` and the latest release. Older releases are not patched.

## Safe harbour

If you act in good faith, stay within this scope, and don't access or change other people's data, we won't take legal action against you for your research.
